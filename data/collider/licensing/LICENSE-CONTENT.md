**Note on idea content.** All ideas in `OC_data/`, `conditions_baseline/`,
`curation/`, and `judge_results/` are raw LLM outputs (Claude Sonnet 4).
Citations embedded inside ideas (case-law references, statute numbers, dates,
named studies, monetary penalties) were generated by the model and have not
been verified. The corpus is published as an artifact of LLM output for
methodological study, not as a body of vetted knowledge. Do not use any
specific idea as legal, financial, medical, or professional advice.
