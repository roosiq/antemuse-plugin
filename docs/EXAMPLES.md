## 2. Six hops later, the walk ends and says what arrived

Command: `/walk`

The dose is six hops and each one is bought by a turn, so a walk cannot finish itself while you are away. When the sixth hop is shown the state ends and the next turn opens with an account:

```text
walk · 6 of 6 hops · design→planning [hyponym] → planning→city planning [hyponym] → city planning→zoning [meronym] → zoning→division [hypernym] → division→separation [hypernym] → separation→avulsion [hyponym]
```

## 3. You keep one concept and drop another

Command: `cockpit · K / T`

The cockpit names the concept under the reticle; `K` keeps it, `T` drops it. A judgement is a sighting in the ledger whose move is the verdict, so the report and the pane's tally count the same rows:

```text
hit rate: 1 kept, 1 trashed · 50.0%
```

## 4. You want to look around yourself

Command: `/explore`

The person chooses the concepts; the plugin only records the picks.
