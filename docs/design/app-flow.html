Antemuse · functional flow

One agent state, six hops, one dose, one account. The person can also open /explore and fly the cockpit themselves: it has no dose or provider and ends only on /return. The walk changes what the agent's turn is made of, serves its hops one at a time, and closes with an account the person actually reads. Columns are lanes, not steps: each one is a different actor, read top to bottom — the loop is the turn in the second lane. Drawn from the code, not the docs.

How a state ends

complete

dose served and seen, at least one turn worked

“The act is done, so the state is over.”

returned

the person types /return

“The frame you were in is gone…”

failed

the source died, or it ran dry having served nothing

“nothing arrived”

explore

no dose, no clock, no provider — only /return

“What they picked is theirs.”

superseded

entering another state drops the first

“left walk; no residue kept” — a known gap
