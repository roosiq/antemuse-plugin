ANTEMUSE · what it's used for

Eight situations where the work
needs a different frame.

Antemuse gives the agent one of five bounded states while you keep working. A state is not a place it goes and not a break: it changes the next replies and what the agent brings back when it ends. Each state has a dose, not a timer, and closes by saying what happened.

5 agent states · walk daydream doomscroll dream unstuck
+ /explore — the map you fly yourself
no model calls on the five states' path
01 · NEW IDEA

Everything you draft sounds like everything else

Two things you already knew surface side by side, the line says what they touch, and the possibility follows from the join.

/daydream → 2 pairs
02 · STUCK

Same problem for an hour, nothing moves

It reads the repository's own history and names one thing to do next, with the evidence behind it — one direction, not a menu.

/unstuck → 1 direction
03 · FEATURES

The roadmap is familiar ground

Six hops away from the current frame, then the day's projects recombine. The path is scenery; the answer comes back changed.

/walk → /dream
04 · NAMING

Naming a thing — product, feature, essay

Roles from two different projects are laid over each other, so the name arrives from outside the vocabulary you were circling.

/dream → 2 recombinations
05 · TIE

Two options, neither convincing

The walk leaves the tie behind on purpose; the reply comes back with a third option rather than a vote between the two.

/walk → 6 hops
06 · RECKONING

“What have I actually been doing all week?”

Two projects are joined on a shared role, using only local commit subjects — never transcripts, never notes.

/dream → 2 recombinations
07 · DISTANCE

Distance, no goal

Three items from a gated public feed, labelled untrusted. Nothing from the page is carried back or turned into a plan.

/doomscroll → 3 items
08 · YOU DRIVE

You want to fly the map yourself

The concept graph as a cockpit: you pick, the plugin records your picks, and /return names them. No model in the loop.

/explore → no provider

What this is not: not a creativity scorer, not a game, not a generative model, not a timer or an automatic break, and not a claim that the ideas are better.
Experimental exploration methods are opt-in and off by default.
Ends are complete · dry · failed · returned. Every ending writes one account and one ledger row.
