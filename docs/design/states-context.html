Antemuse · five states that change the context, and the map you fly yourself

What each bounded state does to a conversation: what it appends to the turn, what it makes the reply do, what the person sees, and how it lets go. `explore` is beside them for contrast — it has no dose and no provider, because the person is the one moving. Every state changes the context, not the message history — the chat log is never rewritten.

walk WALKING6 hops · feeds nothing
Directive only. “Keep the task moving and answer what you were asked. The walk supplies no material… Do not invent a hop.”
Hops are served but never injected — they light the pane and fill the account, and the turn sees none of them.
Work continues at full speed. Replies stay ordinary: no new subject, no material to react to. The state is deliberately invisible in content and visible only in the annotation.

explore EXPLORINGno dose · no provider
Directive only, and it is a prohibition: the person is flying the map; do not name concepts for them, do not offer a route, do not narrate the session. Answer plainly if asked.
The agent is hands-off for as long as the person flies. Their picks are the material, and they reach the next turns through the account, never as an injection.
EXPLORING · open · you fly · you choose
account: 3 shot(s) on 2 concept(s) · bookshelf ×2
ends only on /return
open · note_pick writes the ledger
no machine-side ending at all
What is constant across all five states and explore: the block is appended to the user message of that turn, never to the history; the directive is replaced every turn, so a state cannot accumulate; the annotation is appended to the reply only while the state is active; and when a state ends, the account is posted once and offered again as the next turn's opening. The person's own words are journalled but never quoted back.
